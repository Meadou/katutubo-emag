## Overview
**Manobo** is not the name of one tribe. It is a broad name for many related peoples of Mindanao, from Agusan to Sarangani, in Bukidnon, the Davao provinces and Cotabato. What the name means is uncertain; one common reading is *Manuvu*, "people". Many groups prefer their own names, among them the Ilianen, the Livunganen-Arumanen, the Talaandig, the Higaonon, the Ata and the Matigsalug.

The full story, from Mindanao up the Salug River to the Matigsalug, is told on the map.
