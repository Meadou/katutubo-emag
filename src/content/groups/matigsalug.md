## Overview
"Our tribal name **Matigsalog** is a give away of our origin," the community writes. "It means 'resident from the Salug River'", the river now called the Davao River. The Matigsalug live along its banks and tributaries, in Kitaotao and San Fernando in Bukidnon, in the Marilog district of Davao City, and in Arakan, Cotabato. They are river people who fish and farm.

## Music
A man plays the **kuglung**, a two-string boat lute carved from one log. A woman plays the **saluray**, a bamboo tube zither, and usually sings. They sing stanzas in turn and dance side by side.
